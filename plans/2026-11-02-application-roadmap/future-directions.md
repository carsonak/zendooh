# Future directions and deferred decisions

[Roadmap](overview.md) · [Specification](../../ZENDOOH.md)

These capabilities remain intentional future work or exploration. They are not hidden beta
requirements or authorization to implement them now. Before starting one, prepare a
focused plan with entry gates, slices, acceptance, human actions and the evolution
sections below.

## Scheduled after beta

[Phase 12](12-portable-merge.md) owns portable merging, conflicts and dependency-aware
undo. [Phase 13](13-scoped-sync.md) owns authorized subsets and wireless sync. These
already have phase plans and must use their stated gates rather than treating this
document as a shortcut.

## Investment and Money Market Fund forecasting

**Entry:** G08 forecast contracts, G09 reporting and H03 beta feedback; representative
privacy-safe product evidence and explicit valuation policy before financial
implementation.

Support contributions, withdrawals, distributions, valuations, fees/taxes and assumptions
without conflating capital movement with income or a forecast with an observed return.
Money Market Fund forecasts are explicitly desired. Typed/custom links can group related
records, but a label such as investment, returns or losses does not establish profit.

Resolve yield conventions, compounding, effective dates, valuation currency, tax/fee
policy, missing prices and realized versus unrealized results. Use checked official
product sources when implementing specific products. First deliver a fictional
independently calculated scenario; then a corrected valuation and a
contribution/withdrawal scenario. Acceptance must include rounding/date boundaries, rule
versions, no fabricated postings and reproducibility.

## Hosted, user-requested forecast-rule downloads

**Entry:** H03 and multi-user beta feedback; explicit hosting/publishing ownership and
approval. Beta only links to official terms and supports versioned personal defaults.

Later, allow an optional explicit check/download, potentially prompted when using
forecasts. Do not fetch silently or replace personal defaults. Agents prepare local
package generation, validation, tests and deployment instructions before grouping
hosting/publication actions for the user. Do not publish services or packages merely
because this plan exists.

Resolve authoritative source review, signed package format, trusted key
distribution/rotation, effective dates, compatibility, rollback and update cadence.
Acceptance covers unavailable network, stale/forged/unsupported packages, key rotation,
retained personal overrides and reproducible old forecasts. A successful download does not
independently validate provider terms or authorize a change to historical transactions.

## iOS and platform expansion

**Entry:** H03, stabilized shared workflows, macOS/Xcode access and a dedicated capability
assessment. Preserve shared-domain reuse; validate iOS acquisition through permitted
manual, import or sharing paths rather than assuming Android SMS access.

Resolve supported OS/devices, key storage, encrypted persistence, background behavior,
backup destinations and distribution. First deliver a manual/import/recovery journey with
platform tests. Add sync only after its grants/protocol have cross-platform evidence.
Desktop remains a development host until a separate end-user scope is agreed.

## Rich insights and enrichment

**Entry:** G09 and validated input data for each question; H03 before beta-independent
expansion.

- Advanced charts, spending calendars/velocity, flow diagrams, broader filters and local
  chart/aggregate exports: require reconciled totals, accessible equivalents and
  privacy-safe exports.
- Natural-language questions/retrieval: require traceable report semantics, uncertainty
  and evaluation; cloud inference is not implied.
- Receipts/OCR and personal item-price history: require item quantities/prices and payment
  linking without duplicate expenses; compare like products and keep extraction
  correctable.
- Asset/project profitability: requires attributable costs/earnings and valuation, not
  merely payment labels. Dedicated reimbursement balances and formal owner contributions
  require a further accounting decision and tests before reporting amounts owed or equity.

## Community, sources and reuse

**Entry:** Reliable local learning for community parsers; a dedicated privacy/trust design
before sharing. Other adapters require the shared acquisition and validation contracts.

- Community parser packages must be declarative, reviewed, sanitized, versioned and
  reversible; no executable payloads or automatic trust. Resolve withdrawal and regression
  policies.
- Statements, email, invoices, forwarded text and notifications need independent
  permission, provenance, parsing and deduplication tests. Do not force every adapter into
  streaming.
- Crowdsourced prices require explicit opt-in, minimal contributions, aggregation
  safeguards and no raw financial uploads or workspace linkage by default.
- POS/employee-device reuse must extract demonstrated generic components without
  introducing inventory/fiscal assumptions into the beta ledger.

## Security, lifecycle and public distribution

**Entry:** Evidence from beta and relevant merge/sync phases; explicit product decisions.

Resolve stronger local profile isolation, restricted-edit expansion, multiple
administrators, lost-device/key rotation policies, authorized parser-knowledge sharing,
and retention/history compaction. Do not discard history until undo, replica and backup
guarantees are defined and tested. Define forgetting/deletion effects across replicas and
old backups without promising remote or physical erasure that cannot be enforced.

Public launch needs a separate security review, current platform/store acquisition-policy
validation, signing/release process and brand clearance. Commercial pricing/feature gates
and parent-company branding remain undecided. The repository's existing Apache 2.0 license
is implemented; do not describe it as an unchosen repository license or alter it
incidentally.

## New additions

None beyond the agreed future directions. Record date, requested capability, rationale,
prerequisites and whether it changes beta scope. Do not silently promote future work into
beta.

## Deviations from the original plan

None. Record changes to ordering or boundaries and update the overview/acceptance matrix.

## Learning notes

- Hosting was deliberately deferred until a working beta has multi-user feedback.
- Flexible relationship exploration and complete investment accounting are different
  capabilities.
- Platform reuse needs real permission/storage evidence; shared code alone does not prove
  it.
